# Satellite Tracker

## Default View

**Ambient globe view**
A slowly rotating 3D Earth, with satellites drifting around it in real time.

## Satellites present on globe by default
 
**Indian**
- INSAT-3D - weather/meteorological satellite
- Cartosat-2A - earth observation
- GSAT-30 - communications
- RISAT-2B - radar imaging
- RESOURCESAT-2 - earth observation

**Russian**
- GLONASS-M - navigation
- Meridian - communications
- Elektro-L - weather/meteorological satellite
- Resurs-P 4 - earth observation

**International**
- ISS (International Space Station)

## Features

- **Draggable globe** - Click and drag anywhere on the globe to spin it and look from any angle. Satellites present at this location will be shown.

- **Control Panel**

  - Control Panel contains more advanced features listed below and is opened by a button placed on the top left of the screen

    - **Search bar** 
      - Phase 1 : Typing a satellite's name and filtering the list
      - Phase 2 : AI Recommendation - Describe what you're looking for in plain language and get matching suggested satellites


    - **Filters** - Toggle to show only certain kinds of satellites — weather, GPS, active only or filter by Country, instead of everything at once.
    - **Satellite list** - scrollable, showing name, status, and altitude/orbit type
      - **Saved section** - Lets you bookmark Satellites that appear here and are separated from the main list

- **Click-to-focus** - Clicking a satellite in the list or on the globe highlights its position on the globe and displays path, also moves the Globe to center the satellite on your screen.

- **Orbit trail** - Displays a faint trailing line behind a selected satellite, tracing the path it's already traveled across the globe.

- **Live position accuracy** - Every satellite's position reflects its real, current location continuously recalculated.
